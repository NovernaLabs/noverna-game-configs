# Third-party notices

## UE4SS custom game configs

All games except `games/palworld` come from `zCustomGameConfigs.zip` of [RE-UE4SS v4.0.0](https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases/tag/v4.0.0), unchanged except for the folder layout (store variants moved from `steam/` and `gamepass/` to `ue4ss-steam/` and `ue4ss-gamepass/`, readmes to `NOTES.md`).

The Palworld values come from [RE-UE4SS issue #802](https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/issues/802) (tested offsets posted by Okaetsu).
